## Description: <br>
Guides an agent through invoking the host-side `doca_spcx_cc` CLI to load, parameterize, start, observe, or stop an SPC-X congestion-control algorithm on a BlueField DPA processor against a live RDMA / RoCE fabric, including choosing between SPCX and the established `doca-pcc` surface and diagnosing load or no-effect failures. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

NVIDIA <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External developers, CC researchers, platform operators, and AI agents use this skill to load, evaluate, and troubleshoot custom SPC-X congestion-control algorithms on DPA-capable BlueField devices over non-prod RDMA / RoCE fabrics, and to decide with evidence and a rollback plan whether an algorithm is safe to roll forward to production. <br>
