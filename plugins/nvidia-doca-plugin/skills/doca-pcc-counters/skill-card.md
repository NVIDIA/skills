## Description: <br>
Guides agents in invoking the DOCA PCC Counters tool (`pcc_counters.sh`) to arm (`set`) and read (`query`) the fixed firmware/hardware PCC (Programmable Congestion Control) diagnostic counters (CNP, RTT, WRED-drop, etc.) on a ConnectX / BlueField device via mst and the mlx5 debugfs `diag_cnt` interface, and to diagnose errors such as `Bad Device` or counters stuck at zero. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

NVIDIA <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Network operators, developers, and AI agents use this skill to read a ConnectX-7 / BlueField-3 device's firmware PCC diagnostic counters with `pcc_counters.sh` as evidence for congestion-control investigations (CNP generation, RTT requests/responses, WRED drops), and to troubleshoot the script's device-resolution, arming, and debugfs/permission failures. <br>
