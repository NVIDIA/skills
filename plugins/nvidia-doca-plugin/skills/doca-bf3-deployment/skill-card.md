## Description: <br>
Guides BlueField-3 (BF3) day-1 platform bring-up via the classic RShim/BFB path — pushing a BlueField bundle (BFB) over RShim with bfb-install, bringing up the host-to-DPU TMFIFO management channel, checking RShim daemon state and console, routing DPU-mode selection via mlxconfig, recovering after a BFB push with a six-state BlueField-state classifier, and verifying the install — with destructive steps requiring explicit target-bound confirmation and the doca-hardware-safety skill. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External DOCA operators and developers with a physical BlueField-3, host-side RShim access, a matching DOCA-Host install, and a BFB image use this skill to push a BFB, bring up or recover the TMFIFO channel and RShim console, route DPU-mode changes, recover a DPU that did not come back, and verify the host-side and Arm-side DOCA install. <br>
