## Description: <br>
Guides hands-on DOCA Arg Parser CLI work on a shipped DOCA sample or new DOCA-using app — adding, removing, or renaming flags; wiring `doca_argp_init` → register params → `doca_argp_start` → `doca_argp_destroy` in order; choosing a parameter type from the six-value public enum; preserving the built-in DOCA flags; and debugging `DOCA_ERROR_*` codes returned from `doca_argp_*` calls. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>
