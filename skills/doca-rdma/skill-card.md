## Description: <br>
Guides RDMA data movement on NVIDIA BlueField/ConnectX NICs (RoCE/InfiniBand) using the DOCA RDMA library instead of hand-written libibverbs/rdma_cm, covering context bring-up on a doca_dev, connection methods (RDMA CM, bridge/OOB, or gRPC exchange of doca_rdma_export()), the eleven task types, matching mmap and RDMA permissions, queue and connection sizing, doca_rdma_cap_* queries, and DOCA_ERROR_* diagnosis. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>
