## Description: <br>
Guides launching and supervising a DOCA-linked binary directly on hardware (host x86 with a BlueField NIC over PCIe, or bare-metal on BlueField Arm) and diagnosing bare-metal launch, device-bind, co-tenant isolation, and BlueField lifecycle failures. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Owner
NVIDIA <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
Apache 2.0 <br>
## Use Case: <br>
External DOCA developers and operators who have built a DOCA-linked application binary use this skill to run it directly on hardware (host x86 or BlueField Arm), bind it to the correct PCIe function, NUMA node, CPU set, and IRQs, set up per-tenant isolation, and diagnose bare-metal launch and BlueField lifecycle failures. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>
