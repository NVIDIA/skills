## Description: <br>
Checks if an NVIDIA NIM fits a GPU host or cluster before a pull, never from memory; lists models that fit; picks the NGC or China distributor image, tag and profile. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Owner
NVIDIA <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
CC-BY-4.0 AND Apache 2.0 <br>
## Use Case: <br>
Developers and engineers use this skill to check, from published NIM metadata and read-only host or Kubernetes inspection, whether an NVIDIA NIM fits their GPUs, to list the NIMs that fit, and to pick a pinned NGC or China NIM distributor image, tag and profile before handing off to deployment. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>
