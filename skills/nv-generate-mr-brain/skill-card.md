## Description: <br>
Used for generating synthetic T1, T2, FLAIR, SWI, or MRA brain MRI volumes with NV-Generate-CTMR MR-Brain v1. Not for production training data. <br>

This skill is for research and development only. <br>

## Owner
NVIDIA <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers and engineers use this skill to run the NV-Generate-CTMR rflow-mr-brain v1 workflow through a thin wrapper that stages config overrides, generates synthetic brain MRI NIfTI volumes for a chosen modality, and emits a validated JSON summary for engineering-time development. Not for clinical deployment, clinical interpretation, autonomous diagnosis, regulatory submission, or production training data. <br>
