## Description: <br>
Guides hands-on DOCA AES-GCM work on a BlueField DPU or ConnectX NIC — configuring `doca_aes_gcm_task_encrypt` / `_task_decrypt`, querying `doca_aes_gcm_cap_*` for per-key-type (only `DOCA_AES_GCM_KEY_128` / `_256` — AES-192 not supported) and per-task support, sizing plaintext against the max-buf cap, setting source / destination mmap permissions, validating with a NIST GCMVS or RFC 5288 vector, and debugging DOCA_ERROR_* including the security-critical tag-verification-failed outcome on decrypt, while routing non-GCM AES modes, key management, SHA, and general AEAD background elsewhere. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>
