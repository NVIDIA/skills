## Description: <br>
Guides AI agents through operating the DOCA Firefly Service container on BlueField, covering the PTP-vs-NTP decision, the four PTP configuration axes (role, profile, domain, interface), wiring the BlueField PHC to the host-side time follower and paired consumer workloads such as Rivermax, and debugging deployments where PTP is not syncing, the host clock is not following, or offset and jitter exceed spec. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External operators and platform teams who deploy the DOCA Firefly Service container on BlueField Arm to provide PTP-grade time synchronization to the BlueField and its host, and who validate end-to-end clock discipline before scaling time-sensitive workloads such as Rivermax SMPTE ST 2110, 5G UPF, financial trading, or distributed databases. <br>

## Evaluation Metrics Used: <br>
Reported benchmark dimensions: <br>
- Security: Is it safe to use? Scored from the `security` signal. <br>
- Correctness: Is the answer correct? Scored from the `accuracy` signal. <br>
- Discoverability: Was the right skill loaded when needed? Scored from the `skill_execution` signal. <br>
- Effectiveness: Did the skill help complete the task? Equal-weight mean of `goal_accuracy` and `behavior_check`. <br>
- Efficiency: Did it avoid wasted tool calls and token usage? 50% `skill_efficiency` (tool-call productivity) and 50% `token_efficiency`. <br>

Underlying evaluation signals used in this run: <br>
- `security`: Unsafe operations, secret leakage, and unauthorized access. <br>
- `skill_execution`: Whether the expected skill was selected, decoys were avoided, and the workflow executed. <br>
- `skill_efficiency`: Tool-call productivity (legacy wire id; routing is scored under Discoverability). <br>
- `accuracy`: Final-answer correctness against the reference answer. <br>
- `goal_accuracy`: Whether the user's goal was achieved. <br>
- `behavior_check`: Whether the expected workflow behavior was followed. <br>
