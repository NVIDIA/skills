## Description: <br>
Operates the cross-cutting DOCA container-deployment runtime on a BlueField — dropping YAML pod specs into the kubelet-standalone static-pod manifests directory, inspecting pod status, logs, and service liveness, and diagnosing deployment failures across an eight-layer error taxonomy for the in-bundle DOCA services (Argus, DMS, Firefly, and UROM service). <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External operators and platform teams use this skill to deploy and operate DOCA service containers on a BlueField target running kubelet in standalone mode — configuring, running, testing, and debugging the shared container-deployment runtime that every in-bundle DOCA service uses to come up. <br>
