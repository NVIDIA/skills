## Description: <br>
Guides running an in-bundle DOCA service (Argus, DMS, Firefly, or UROM service) as a container on a BlueField via kubelet standalone watching a static-pod manifests directory, covering YAML pod-spec drop, kubelet status, ENTRYPOINT logs, per-service liveness, smoke-before-bulk validation, and an eight-layer error taxonomy (pod-spec, scheduling, image pull, runtime, mount, network, version, host) for diagnosing failed deployments. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

NVIDIA <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External operators and platform teams who deploy DOCA service containers on a BlueField with DOCA installed use this skill to drop documented pod specs into the kubelet static-pod manifests directory, verify that pods and the services inside them come up healthy, and diagnose Pending, ImagePullBackOff, CrashLoopBackOff, or not-ready deployments, routing per-service config, DOCA install, and full Kubernetes-cluster questions to their owning skills. <br>
