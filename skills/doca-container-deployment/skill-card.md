## Description: <br>
Guides running an in-bundle DOCA service (Argus, DMS, Firefly, or UROM service) as a container on a BlueField using kubelet standalone watching a static-pod manifests directory, covering YAML pod-spec drop, kubelet status, ENTRYPOINT logs, per-service liveness, smoke-before-bulk, and an eight-layer error taxonomy (pod-spec, scheduling, image pull, runtime, mount, network, version, host). <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External operators, developers, and platform teams who deploy DOCA service containers on BlueField targets use this skill to configure and drop pod specs, verify deployments through the smoke-before-bulk loop, and troubleshoot pod and container lifecycle failures with a layered error taxonomy. <br>
