## Description: <br>
Runs standard or fixed-channel softmax finetuning of NV-Segment-CT VISTA3D on CT NIfTI image/label datasets, with optional MONAI-native MLflow tracking and checkpoint evidence. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Developers and medical imaging engineers use this skill to run smoke-scale or dataset finetuning of the NV-Segment-CT VISTA3D model on their own CT NIfTI image/label data (including the upstream fixed-channel softmax workflow and MSD Task06 sanity reproduction) by wrapping the upstream MONAI bundle entrypoint; it is not for clinical validation, clinical deployment, or diagnosis. <br>

## Evaluation Metrics Used: <br>
Reported benchmark dimensions: <br>
- Security: Is it safe to use? Scored from `security` (100%). <br>
- Correctness: Is the answer correct? Scored from `accuracy` (100%). <br>
- Discoverability: Was the right skill loaded when needed? Scored from `skill_execution` (100%). <br>
- Effectiveness: Did the skill help complete the task? Scored from `goal_accuracy` (50%) + `behavior_check` (50%). <br>
- Efficiency: Did it avoid wasted tool calls and token usage? Scored from `skill_efficiency` (50%) + `token_efficiency` (50%). <br>

Underlying evaluation signals used in this run: <br>
- `security`: Unsafe operations, secret leakage, and unauthorized access. <br>
- `skill_execution`: Whether the expected skill was selected, decoys were avoided, and the workflow executed. <br>
- `skill_efficiency`: Tool-call productivity (legacy wire id; routing is scored under Discoverability). <br>
- `accuracy`: Final-answer correctness against the reference answer. <br>
- `goal_accuracy`: Whether the user's goal was achieved. <br>
- `behavior_check`: Whether the expected workflow behavior was followed. <br>
- `token_efficiency`: Actual uncached prompt plus completion usage (50% of Efficiency). <br>
