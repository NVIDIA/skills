## Description: <br>
Guides invocation of doca_flow_dpa_perf on DPA-capable hardware (ConnectX-7 minimum, ConnectX-8 recommended, or BlueField-3) to measure rule update and disable rates on the DPA-offloaded DOCA Flow path, covering the active/passive device split, workload-shape axes, Kops/sec iteration stats, and the optional self-test. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External operators, performance engineers, DOCA Flow application developers, and AI agents use this skill to produce defensible measurements of DPA-offloaded DOCA Flow rule update and disable rates on DPA-capable hardware, and to diagnose zero-ops, hung, or failed self-test runs. <br>
