## Description: <br>
Operate NVIDIA DOCA Management Service on a BlueField, Arm/x86 host, or Kubernetes pod: from DOCA 3.5 the local `dms-cli` (one command per YANG path, no server), and the `dmsd` + `dmspe` service: choose deployment and authentication, configure `-allowed_users` and `dmsgroup`, use gNMI Get/Set, run supported gNOI workflows, and debug frontend/backend failures, with destructive operations (reboot, OS install, factory-reset, managed-file deletion) gated on target-bound explicit confirmation. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External operators, platform teams, and developers who deploy and operate DOCA Management Service use this skill to manage NVIDIA BlueField networking platforms or ConnectX SmartNICs, locally with `dms-cli` or remotely over gRPC (gNMI/gNOI) via `dmsd` + `dmspe`, including choosing deployment shape and authentication mode, issuing configuration and operational requests, and debugging frontend/backend errors and logs. <br>
