## Description: <br>
Operate NVIDIA DOCA Management Service on a BlueField, Arm/x86 host, or Kubernetes pod, using the local `dms-cli` (DOCA 3.5+) or the `dmsd` + `dmspe` service to choose deployment and authentication, configure `-allowed_users` and `dmsgroup`, use gNMI Get/Set/Subscribe, run supported gNOI workflows with target-bound confirmation for destructive operations, and debug frontend/backend failures. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

External operators and platform teams who deploy and operate NVIDIA DOCA Management Service to manage BlueField networking platforms or ConnectX SmartNICs, locally via `dms-cli` or from a centralized control plane via gNMI/gNOI, including daemon bring-up, authentication mode selection, Kubernetes deployment wiring, and frontend-vs-backend debugging. <br>
