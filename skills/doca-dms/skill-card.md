## Description: <br>
Operate NVIDIA DOCA Management Service on a BlueField, Arm/x86 host, or Kubernetes pod — choosing deployment and authentication, running the local dms-cli or the dmsd/dmspe gRPC service, issuing gNMI Get/Set/Subscribe and supported gNOI workflows, and debugging frontend/backend failures. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External operators and platform teams who deploy and operate DMS use this skill to manage NVIDIA BlueField networking platforms and ConnectX SmartNICs from a centralized control plane — selecting a deployment shape and authentication mode, wiring dmsd/dmspe, issuing gNMI/gNOI operations, and debugging DMS request failures. <br>
